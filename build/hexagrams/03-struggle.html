I Ching Hexagram 3

䷂ Struggle

Struggle / Sprouting / Difficulty at the Beginning

Chinese屯
Pinyinzhūn
Binary100010

Reading

Rain and thunder arise—there will be development and creativity.

Like walking into a storm, there will be struggle progressing, but well-prepared and firm, the journey through the tempest can be managed, to advantage. Do not take things lightly—this is movement in peril. There is much irregularity and confusion, but where there is risk, there is often reward—whatever begins, develops, and whatever develops, will bear fruit. Carefully manage affairs, like an artisan weaving the warp of a loom. This is done by setting up a ruler who can understand and manage the course, without abandoning the Dao. Remember, promoting strength is the effect of not having it—yang energy is latent here. When primal energy appears, this is called "living midnight." Do not be reckless and desirous, allowing the sure driver to hold the reigns, and the latent energy will reappear in time.

Changing Lines

Line 1: When advancement is difficult, stick to correct paths, and a good coach.

The effective ruler sets up a good foundation, and nurtures primal energy. When people avoid extremes, staying the course by humbling themselves to balance, they are praised for following their noble character.

Line 2: Overburdened and mastered by the struggle, one is obliged to return.

Circumstances reject advancement and union, but by upholding virtue, the course will reset itself in time. Be adaptable, refine yourself, and wait. Seeking immediate solutions is unleashing newborn yang energy, to injury.

Line 3: Follow the deer without a ranger, and find yourself in the midst of the thick forest.

Becoming lost is an extreme situation—a wiser person knows the hidden risks of dangerous pursuit, withdrawing from the chase before disaster. Do not be arbitrary. Do not rush.

Line 4: The chariot is turned back, almost to retreat, but the aim is still true.

Having nurtured flexibility, and properly orienting oneself, neither the Dao, nor the yang energy, is lost. Stay true at the threshold, by seeking solutions appropriate to the time.

Line 5: Advancement may become expensive.

Stay firm and correct, and there can some good fortune, even if things go awry. Forestall great matters, for danger is still imminent. Wait for the proper time to escape the struggle. Nonstriving will be beneficial.

Line 6: The storm defeats you, and your horses weep tears of blood.

Continuing in this state is great suffering. Weak and helpless, without a whip, turned back by difficulty, it is most necessary to come to understand the medicine of the time. Do not experience struggle where there is none.
